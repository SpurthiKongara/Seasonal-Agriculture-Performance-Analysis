# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## **📌 Project Overview**

**Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis** is a data analytics project that investigates how agricultural performance varies across different seasons. The project analyzes farm-level agricultural data to identify meaningful **patterns, trends, relationships, and seasonal variations** in farming activities and outcomes.

The analysis considers factors such as **crop yield, production, environmental conditions, resource usage, irrigation methods, and economic performance** across different seasons and regions.

## **🎯 Objectives**

- Understand and explore the agricultural dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
- Study seasonal variations in environmental conditions.
- Examine resource usage and water efficiency.
- Compare crop-wise and season-wise performance.
- Analyze regional/state-wise agricultural performance.
- Identify relationships between agricultural factors and outcomes.
- Perform statistical analysis to identify significant seasonal differences.
- Generate evidence-based findings and recommendations.

## **📊 Dataset**

The dataset contains **4,000 agricultural records and 28 variables**, covering:

- Farm and geographical information
- Crop and seasonal information
- Farm area
- Rainfall, temperature, humidity, and sunlight
- Soil characteristics
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Irrigation methods
- Crop yield and production
- Market price
- Cost, revenue, and profit
- Water usage and water efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

## **📈 Key Insights**

- **Kharif** showed the strongest overall seasonal performance in terms of average yield, production, and profit.
- **Zaid** recorded lower average yield and profitability compared with the other seasons.
- Environmental conditions such as rainfall, temperature, humidity, and sunlight varied considerably across seasons.
- Water usage and water-use efficiency showed noticeable seasonal differences.
- Crop performance varied significantly, with some crops performing consistently better than others.
- Regional performance was not uniform across seasons.
- Statistical analysis provided evidence of significant differences in yield distributions across seasons.

## **💡 Recommendations**

- Consider seasonal conditions when planning crop cultivation.
- Improve water-use efficiency, particularly during high water-demand periods.
- Evaluate crop profitability along with yield before making cultivation decisions.
- Review consistently loss-making crop-season combinations.
- Consider regional differences when developing agricultural strategies.
- Use multiple environmental, resource, and economic factors rather than relying on a single indicator.
